# QCore Labs QPIX — Multi-Source Source Audit (November 18, 2026)

**Scope:** All 12 Eastern-date NBA finals; original ESPN event archive, PBPStats, and Basketball-Reference. **Status:** research/source enrichment only; not an officially rescored QPIX run.

## Verified coverage

- 12 of 12 final games reconciled by local date, home/away pairing and final scores. ESPN event IDs and NBA GameIds retained separately.
- ESPN original full-detail archive: 5,754 events.
- ESPN original derived box records: 279, including **Isaac Jones (DET, 0 minutes)**; the original QPIX scored dataset correctly has **278 minutes-positive players**.
- Basketball-Reference individual `off_rtg`/`def_rtg`: **279/279** ESPN records matched by player and team; **278/278** scored players. BRef source is individual Dean Oliver estimates, not NBA lineup/on-court ratings.
- PBPStats player game records: **159/279**, of which **158/278** are scored players; successfully matched games were HOU@IND, MIL@MIA, PHI@ORL, CHI@BKN, WAS@TOR, DET@NOP and ATL@OKC.
- PBPStats explicit player `SecondChancePoints`: **70** named player observations among the matched records. Missing key is retained as **null** pending a confirmed zero-suppression policy.
- ESPN, PBPStats and BRef player and game namespaces remain separate; confidence and provenance are recorded in the archived research report.

## Source nuances

- PBPStats player records are nested at `stats.Away.FullGame[]` and `stats.Home.FullGame[]`. The `stats` object is *not* itself an array and must not be naively summed over period records.
- Basketball-Reference uses page/row aliases `BRK` for Brooklyn and `PHO` for Phoenix, unlike ESPN/PBPStats `BKN` and `PHX`. ESPN also uses `WSH`, `NO`, `GS`, `UTAH` instead of `WAS`, `NOP`, `GSW`, `UTA`.
- PBPStats intermittently timed out or returned 502 on the remaining five games. Its detailed possession endpoint returned HTTP 500 for a correctly parameterized Dallas test; don't infer missing data from response failure.
- PBPStats gives `SecondChancePoints` and live/dead-ball turnovers, but **does not expose explicitly named player fast-break or paint-point totals** in the inspected contract.
- ESPN provides team fast-break/paint totals and every original scoring play, but not directly categorized player fields.

## Quality gates

1. Keep original `generate-report.js` untouched; never replace QPIX RAW with a companion rating.
2. Do not treat PBPStats missing keys as observed zeros.
3. Use compatible BRef individual ORtg/DRtg only after player identity and methodology verification; keep QCore estimates alongside observed ratings for audit.
4. Keep `fast_break_points`, `points_in_paint`, and unsourced `second_chance_points` **null/unverified**, never silently zero.
5. Continue shot- and possession-centered transition classification as experimental, comparing individual attribution with official NBA percentages and team totals with ESPN.
6. The standalone multi-source enriched output preserves original RAW, QPIX-R and all category contributions, with a separate `provider_comparison` field. It has not been promoted to production scoring.
7. The daily workflow should cache source responses and limit provider retries rather than block ESPN collection.
