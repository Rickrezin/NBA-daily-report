This is a PROVISIONAL SHADOW report, not an official certified QPIX release. Compatible individual provider ratings remain unavailable, and player-level points in the paint, fast-break points and second-chance points were not verified. Both ranked lists require strictly more than eight minutes, without an FGA cutoff.
