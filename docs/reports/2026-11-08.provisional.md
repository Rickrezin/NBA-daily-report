THE QPIX DIFFERENCE

The same scoring engine that puts 43-point Reaves third places 25-point Flagg first and 12-point Robinson fourth. That is the purpose of QPIX: value shooting efficiency, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, possession protection and game context together rather than using points alone as the scoreboard for individual impact. A steal that leads to an assist remains a steal and an assist — two separate contributions in the same possession.
