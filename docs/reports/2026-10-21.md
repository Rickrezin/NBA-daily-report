**45 points doesn't automatically guarantee the highest performance rating.** Edwards and Jerome scored the same number of points, but QPIX separated their full-game production, efficiency and impact. Quickley and Sabonis illustrate the value of facilitation and rebounding.

QPIX™ evaluates base production, true-shooting efficiency, individual offensive/defensive impact, defensive activity, playmaking quality, offensive context, gravity, game environment, multi-category bonuses, fatigue, momentum, and second-unit contribution.

**RAW QPIX determines the official ranking.** QPIX-R (free-throw normalization) and QPIX-SFA (former-team familiarity) are companion measures and do not reorder RAW rankings.
